Who we protect: seniors first, then everyone

core

Digital arrest, fake bank/KYC, relative in trouble, romance.

Working adults

Investment, courier/parcel, e-commerce. 85.2% of Singapore’s 2025 victims were under 65.

Young adults

Job/task scams, fake sellers, loan apps. Job scams: S$123.5M in Singapore.

Teens

Sextortion and gaming giveaways. 11.3% of Australian adolescents report sexual extortion.

One engine detects the coercion state of a conversation, so the same product extends to every age group by changing the audience profile and who the guardian is.

How it works

1. Listen
Speakerphone, Google Meet tab, or demo call. 16 kHz audio from the browser.
→
2. Understand
gemini-3.8-live transcribes and reasons about coercion signals. Calls raise_alert with signals + exact quotes.
→
3. Warn
Calm whisper in the user’s language. Big “Hang up & call family” button.
→
4. Act
Case Agent (Gemini Flash function calling): official advisory, guardian alert, cool-off hold, complaint draft for 1930.
Frame Check
Meet video stills → visual cues (uniform, fake warrant). Cues, never a deepfake verdict.
Scam Check
Screenshot, audio recording, pasted text or a question → structured verdict, red flags, safe reply, follow-up chat.
Guardian console
Family phone gets realtime alerts and approves the hold.

Demo flow

  1. A teammate plays a fake CBI officer on Google Meet: parcel, money laundering, “stay on video, tell no one”, “transfer to the RBI account”.
  2. Signals stack up with quotes; meter → STOP; Hindi whisper; video cue chip.
  3. Guardian’s phone buzzes → approves the hold → complaint draft appears.
  4. A genuine bank callback triggers nothing.
  5. Scam Check: WhatsApp task-job screenshot, a voice note, and a Teen-mode sextortion DM.

How much could be avoided?

12%
Conservative: NAB (Australia) real-time in-app scam prompts led customers to abandon 12% of flagged payments.
up to 94%
Ceiling: a Nottingham lab experiment found confirm-the-action friction cut fraudulent payments by up to 94% (warnings alone: 18%).

Applied to published loss pools (illustrative, not a forecast): 12% of Japan’s 2025 fake-police losses ≈ ¥12B a year; 12% of India’s 2024 digital-arrest losses ≈ ₹230 crore a year. India’s 1930 rapid-reporting system already helped save ~₹8,189 crore by Dec 2025, showing speed matters. Our instant complaint draft feeds that channel.

Safety, privacy & feasibility

  • No raw audio or video stored: only risk level, signal names, short quotes and the complaint draft.
  • Guardian approves any hold; nothing is filed automatically; we show the exact words behind every alert.
  • Free-tier prototype: production would use paid-tier or Vertex AI data controls, bank/telco partnerships and consent flows.
  • Runs on Firebase’s free plan today; cost per protected call is a few minutes of Live audio.
